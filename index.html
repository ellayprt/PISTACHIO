About Pistachio

Pistachio (Pistacia vera) is a small tree native to Central Asia and the Middle East. Its edible seeds — the pistachios we love — are packed with healthy fats, protein, fiber, and antioxidants.

They are one of the oldest flowering nut trees, and are cherished for their distinctive green hue and subtly sweet, earthy flavour.
